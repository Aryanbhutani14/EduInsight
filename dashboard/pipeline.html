2
Module 2 — Distributed Preprocessing
Tanmay Kandwal
  • Spark job (8 partitions): jieba tokenization + 794-word Chinese stopword list + a reviewed spoken-lecture filler list over every caption
  • Concept-noise filter as a Spark transform: 786 generic ≤2-character concepts dropped, 26,766 kept
  • Distributed concept↔video join and a corpus term-statistics aggregation (tf, df, idf, tf-idf)
  • All outputs written back to HDFS as Parquet under /eduinsight/processed/
Deliverable: preprocessing module, run end to end in 29 s on the CS subset
Verified: 5.94M raw tokens → 1.80M kept (69.6% removed), 61,094-term vocabulary (49% appear in only one video), 3,938 of 4,071 videos linked to clean concepts. Before/after examples and the TF-IDF term chart are on the CS Subset & EDA page.
Done
3
Module 3 — Embedding & Feature Engineering
Tanmay Sangwan
  • Verified the model's real input limit (128 tokens), so each lecture's captions are split into chunks and the chunk vectors averaged into one vector per video
  • Spark chunks the captions; the RTX 4050 encodes them with paraphrase-multilingual-mpnet-base-v2 (fp16, 2 Spark partitions)
  • Chunk vectors and per-video vectors written back to HDFS as Parquet under /eduinsight/processed/
Deliverable: embedding module — 4,071 video vectors (768-d) from 48,944 chunks in 123 s
Verified: videos from the same course are closer than videos from different courses (cosine 0.638 vs 0.483), and a video's nearest neighbour is in its own course 72.9% of the time. English queries such as "TCP congestion control" retrieve the right Chinese lectures. Known weak spots ("dynamic programming", "linked list") are reported on the CS Subset & EDA page.
Done
4
Module 4 — Similarity & Clustering
Tanmay Sangwan
  • Spark MLlib K-means (cosine distance) over the video vectors; k=12 chosen from a sweep of 9 values by silhouette score
  • Distributed Spark job (broadcast + mapPartitions) finds the 10 most similar videos for every video
  • Cross-course redundancy: candidate pairs checked against real word overlap and read by hand
  • t-SNE 2-D map of all 4,071 videos; results written to HDFS as Parquet
Deliverable: clustering module — 12 topic clusters, a similarity table, and redundancy findings, in 52 s
Verified: every cluster mixes at least 9 courses (topics, not course boundaries). 447 cross-course video pairs score ≥0.90 and are the same topic taught by different lecturers (e.g. IP addressing in two networking courses). Limits: silhouette is low (~0.11) and clusters from other random seeds agree only moderately (ARI 0.57–0.65). Details and the cluster map are on the CS Subset & EDA page.
