MOOCCubeX: the corpus behind EduInsight

A Tsinghua University (THU-KEG) dataset of Chinese MOOC lecture videos, transcribed captions, and machine-extracted concepts — chosen over AVLectures (451GB, impractical for a semester project). Every number on this page comes from files actually downloaded and verified, not documentation claims.

Variety: what's actually in this corpus

The dataset spans 82 disciplines, not just Computer Science, and is Chinese-native — both facts shape the pipeline design (multilingual embeddings, not translation).

The platform (XuetangX) is Chinese-native. The 10.1% non-Chinese-titled courses are mostly Tsinghua's international/English-taught offerings — not enough to be the primary corpus. Design decision: jieba tokenization + a multilingual sentence-transformer, so an English query like "dynamic programming" matches Chinese lecture content directly — no full-corpus translation needed.
